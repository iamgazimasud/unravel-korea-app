# Changelog

All notable changes to the app. Versions match `version` in `app.json`.

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

First Android release.

- Day-by-day trip planner with closed-day warnings, reordering and share links.
- Today mode: next stop, subway directions, Naver Map / KakaoMap, taxi card, tick-off.
- 32 curated spots with search, categories and saved spots.
- Read tab: Unravel Korea guides (WordPress) with categories, search and paging; Seoul Sip posts (beehiiv).
- In-app Seoul Sip signup via the `server/` function, with web fallback.
- Toolkit: Korean phrases with audio, emergency numbers, travel tips, privacy policy.
- Works offline: trip and recently loaded articles are stored on the device.
