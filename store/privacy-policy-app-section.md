## The Unravel Korea app (Android)

This section covers the **Unravel Korea: Trip Planner** app published on Google Play by Unravel Korea.

**What stays on your phone.** Your trip plan (days, places, dates and what you have ticked off) is stored
only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you choose to
share a trip, the app creates a link that you send yourself; it contains only the trip's name, dates, day
names and places.

**Newsletter (optional).** If you subscribe to Seoul Sip from inside the app, the email address you type is
sent over an encrypted connection to our newsletter provider, beehiiv, for the sole purpose of sending you the
newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email, or contact us to have your address
deleted.

**Content we load.** To show travel guides and newsletter issues, the app downloads public articles from
unravelkorea.com and seoulsip.online. Links you open (for example maps, bookings or articles) open in your
browser or in that service's app, under that service's own privacy policy. Some booking links are affiliate
links.

**What we do not collect.** The app has no account or login, no ads and no analytics or tracking. It does not
access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13.
